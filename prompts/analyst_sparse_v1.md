## Sparse-evidence route

This buyer has limited relevant deal history. Treat the acquisition thesis as a
hypothesis supported by the available evidence, not as a demonstrated strategy.
Explain the evidence limitation in the fit thesis and conviction justification.
Keep the code-assigned conviction level unchanged.

Use adjacent-sector activity when it can clarify a plausible connection to the
target. Distinguish those adjacent deals from direct sector precedents. Retrieve
closed comparable deals for valuation even when the buyer has no direct precedent.
Do not turn missing evidence into a claim that the buyer has never made a deal.

The primary grounding, risk, numeric-claim, and data-delimiter rules still apply.
