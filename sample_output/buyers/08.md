# 8. Blackstone

Financial Sponsor · Score 0.569 · Medium conviction




## Acquirer overview

Blackstone is a large financial sponsor with broad healthcare deal activity across Revenue Cycle, Dental, Health Insurance, Medical Devices, Physician Groups, Home Health, and Healthcare Services\. Across its observed transactions, the firm has closed the large majority of pursued deals, reflecting a functioning, disciplined execution process\. Blackstone's presence spans a wide range of deal sizes and structures \(LBOs, bolt\-ons, platform investments, minority stakes\), signaling flexible deal structuring capability\. This scale and structuring flexibility, however, reflects historical activity, not a current stated intent to pursue this specific target\.

## Strategic fit

Blackstone has directly invested in Healthcare Services businesses pursuing geographic expansion, which aligns with this target's stated growth tag\. The target's margin profile sits within the range Blackstone has underwritten previously in this same sector, suggesting the firm has evaluated similarly\-margined assets and could see a margin\-improvement path here too\. That said, Blackstone's overall sector\-fit signal for Healthcare Services specifically is comparatively soft next to its stronger footprint in adjacent healthcare subsectors \(Revenue Cycle, Dental, Health Insurance\), so this should be treated as a plausible but unproven thesis rather than a demonstrated specialization\. Size compatibility is also only moderate: Blackstone's typical deals skew both much larger and, in some cases, much smaller than this target, so check\-size fit for a private, regionally\-focused asset of this scale warrants further validation\.

## Valuation context · Closed transactions

Two Closed Healthcare Services comps near the target's size band provide valuation anchors\. Crest Care Partners \(Advent International\) closed at 9\.3x EV/EBITDA and 3\.36x EV/Revenue, with a margin profile modestly below the target\. Quality Medical Group \(TPG Capital\) closed at 11\.2x EV/EBITDA and 2\.99x EV/Revenue, with a margin nearly identical to the target's\. Both are financial\-sponsor\-led Healthcare Services transactions of broadly comparable scale, offering a reasonable read on likely entry multiples, though neither involves Blackstone directly and buyer\-specific pricing discipline may differ\. Sector\-wide, the broader Healthcare Services population shows a median EV/EBITDA of 13\.6x and EV/Revenue of 2\.7x, which brackets these two comps and supports their use as reference points despite the limited sample\.

## Risks

- **Execution risk · evidence**: Blackstone has withdrawn transactions previously, including in healthcare\-adjacent sectors, indicating deal completion is not assured even with sponsor commitment\. A withdrawal risk could stall or terminate this process late in diligence\. [MA\-2024\-0347](../index.html#evidence-7ab2ec69cd9f61f8cdf02d22af1fb8762761e655fc7a5d440e7bd0d18d8b0c82) [MA\-2020\-0040](../index.html#evidence-a06c89eb45df5edcf58122149b690e145d8037a508f438ddff6ac5c6fdbb52dc) 
- **Financing capacity · judgment**: Blackstone's historical deal sizes vary widely, and its scale in larger transactions does not by itself confirm appetite or committed capital for a target of this size\. Diligence should confirm current fund allocation and deal\-size mandate fit\. 

## Conviction · Medium

Two directly relevant closed Healthcare Services precedents with matching geographic\-expansion and margin characteristics support a plausible thesis, but weaker sector\-specific fit and moderate size alignment temper confidence\. The presence of prior withdrawn deals adds execution uncertainty that diligence must address before this can move beyond a hypothesis\.
