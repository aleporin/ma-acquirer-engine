# 7. Vista Equity

Financial Sponsor · Score 0.574 · Medium conviction




## Acquirer overview

Vista Equity is a financial sponsor with a broad closed\-deal history spanning Healthcare Services, Home Health/Hospice, Behavioral Health, Pharma/Biotech, and Medical Devices\. Across its full observed history, the firm has closed a majority of pursued transactions \(completion\_rate value 0\.79, evidence\_id stat:completion\_rate:Vista%20Equity\), though a portion of past processes were withdrawn prior to close\. Vista's median pricing discipline in past deals \(ev\_ebitda\_multiple 13\.5x, evidence\_id stat:median\_ev\_ebitda\_multiple:Vista%20Equity; ev\_revenue\_multiple 2\.61x, evidence\_id stat:median\_ev\_revenue\_multiple:Vista%20Equity\) is broadly consistent with healthcare services norms\. This activity reflects historical patterns only, not a confirmed current intent to pursue this target\.

## Strategic fit

This target's margin\-improvement and geographic\-expansion profile aligns with recurring themes in Vista's healthcare dealmaking, where the sponsor has repeatedly pursued regional platform consolidation and margin uplift theses in services businesses\. Vista has direct experience owning and integrating Healthcare Services companies at comparable EBITDA margin levels, which supports underwriting confidence on operating improvement plans\. However, sector\-fit signals suggest Vista's core reps are more concentrated in adjacent healthcare verticals \(hospice, behavioral health, biotech\) rather than pure Healthcare Services, so the fit here should be viewed as plausible but not a core specialty\. Geographic fit is only moderate, meaning regional footprint overlap with Vista's existing portfolio is uncertain and should be diligenced directly\.

Two closed Healthcare Services comparables in a similar size range to this target provide useful pricing anchors\. Quality Medical Group \(TPG Capital\) traded at 11\.2x EV/EBITDA and 2\.99x EV/Revenue with an EBITDA margin close to this target's profile, offering the closest comparability on both size and margin\. Crest Care Partners \(Advent International\) traded at 9\.3x EV/EBITDA and 3\.36x EV/Revenue, providing a lower\-multiple reference point at a somewhat larger deal size\. Both comps involve financial\-sponsor buyers, similar to Vista, supporting relevance, though neither involves Vista itself, and margin/growth mix differs enough that these should be treated as directional benchmarks rather than precise pricing guides\.

## Risks

- **Execution risk · evidence**: Vista has withdrawn from prior Healthcare Services processes at a similar deal size to this target, indicating that signed or advanced negotiations do not guarantee closing and diligence should stress\-test deal certainty\. [MA\-2023\-0221](../index.html#evidence-0e42ce5c2779396b7d102fd0633b9dd35ca8a5c49c59fef621f715c20a65007f)
- **Fund cycle · judgment**: Vista's sector\-fit and tag\-fit signals for this specific target profile are comparatively weak relative to its overall platform, suggesting the acquisition thesis may be opportunistic rather than core to current fund strategy, and appetite could shift during a live process\.

## Conviction · Medium

Vista has demonstrated closed activity in this exact sector at comparable margin levels, giving credible operating familiarity, but weaker sector\-fit and geography\-fit signals plus a history of withdrawn Healthcare Services deals at similar scale temper confidence\. Diligence should confirm current fund appetite and deal\-certainty commitment before treating this as a high\-conviction buyer\.
