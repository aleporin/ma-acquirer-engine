# 10. New Mountain Capital

Financial Sponsor · Score 0.565 · Medium conviction




## Acquirer overview

New Mountain Capital is a financial sponsor with a broad healthcare deal history, spanning many transactions across sub\-sectors and a wide range of check sizes\. Its overall completion rate on tracked deals is moderate, and its median disclosed multiples sit in a healthcare\-typical range\. Recent deal cadence has stayed active, but the platform's engagement spans several distinct healthcare niches rather than a single sub\-sector focus, so historical activity signals capability more than a specific current intent toward this target\.

## Strategic fit

This target's profile — regional, private\-owned, margin\-improvement and geographic\-expansion oriented — is a plausible but not proven match for New Mountain\. The firm has repeatedly pursued geographic\-expansion theses across its healthcare platforms, and its historical size range comfortably covers this target's scale, suggesting financing and structuring familiarity at this check size\. However, most of New Mountain's closed healthcare precedents sit in Behavioral Health and Physician Groups rather than the broader Healthcare Services category the target occupies, and the deal most analogous in scale and sub\-sector proximity did not close\. Given this, the fit thesis should be treated as a hypothesis to validate in diligence rather than a demonstrated sector strategy, particularly on how the buyer would approach margin improvement in this specific services niche\.

## Valuation context · Closed transactions

Two Closed Healthcare Services comps in a similar size range to the target provide external valuation anchors, since New Mountain's own multiple history is concentrated in adjacent sub\-sectors rather than this exact category\. Quality Medical Group, close in margin profile to the target, closed at 11\.2x EV/EBITDA and 2\.99x EV/Revenue\. Crest Care Partners, larger and lower\-margin, closed at 9\.3x EV/EBITDA and 3\.36x EV/Revenue\. Both used geographic\-expansion rationale similar to the target, supporting comparability, though neither is the buyer's own transaction, and margin and deal\-type differences mean these should be treated as directional benchmarks rather than precise pricing guides\.

## Risks

- **Execution risk · evidence**: New Mountain's closest sub\-sector and size analogue to this target ultimately withdrew rather than closing, indicating deals with similar profiles for this buyer have faced execution obstacles that could recur here\. [MA\-2024\-0216](../index.html#evidence-4a21dbf58c22644af188554f8a68febdd002e42ec9b4704f28b9be4396bb841a) 
- **Financing capacity · judgment**: While New Mountain has closed very large healthcare transactions historically, its scale of past activity does not by itself confirm current capital availability or appetite for a deal at this target's size, so financing capacity should be independently verified in diligence\. 

## Conviction · Medium

The buyer has plausible size and geography compatibility and some adjacent\-sector precedent supporting a geographic\-expansion thesis, which justifies more than low conviction\. However, weak direct sector\-fit and tag\-fit signals, combined with a withdrawn outcome on the most comparable prior deal, prevent a higher conviction level until sub\-sector and execution risks are cleared in diligence\.
